AntifaWatch, A/I and the Crozier trail

Preserved social posts add an earlier public node to the A/I chronology and document later direct contact between Hudson Crozier and AntifaWatch. They do not, by themselves, prove that AntifaWatch supplied Crozier's original reporting or influenced the federal designation.

Method note: this entry separates what the screenshots establish from what they merely make worth investigating. The distinction is tedious and necessary. Evidence does not improve when humans yell at it.

July 16, 2025

AntifaWatch publicly identifies A/I as shared technical infrastructure.

In a preserved X post, @AntifaWatch tells its audience that web services supporting projects it was tracking were provided by A/I / Autistici/Inventati, identifying the Italian association and describing it as infrastructure supporting antifascist projects internationally.

The date matters. This public infrastructure framing predates Hudson Crozier's October 16, 2025 Daily Caller News Foundation investigation by roughly three months.

Another AntifaWatch post explicitly links the infrastructure theory to foreign-terrorist designation strategy.

A second preserved crop says Rose City Counter-Info, Abolition Media, Scenes from Stop Cop City and an Alvarado-shooting support project were using services from an Italian antifascist tech collective. It then argues that, if the administration was serious about foreign-terrorist designations, foreign technical infrastructure was a place to start.

Because the crop does not display a publication date, Sabot is not assigning one. It is preserved as evidence of the framing, not used to establish chronology beyond what the screenshot itself supports.

August 28, 2026

After the designation, AntifaWatch points back to its July 2025 A/I work.

Two days after the State Department designation, @AntifaWatch posted that the group it had highlighted in July 2025 had now been designated a Specially Designated Global Terrorist and referred again to NoBlogs.

This is useful corroboration of the account's own public research timeline. It is not evidence that government officials relied on that research.

August 30, 2026 · 4:00 PM displayed

Crozier publicly says he emailed and DMed AntifaWatch and asks for more A/I research.

In a public reply preserved in the same screenshot as the July 2025 AntifaWatch post, Hudson Crozier says he sent AntifaWatch an email and direct message, says he had been covering A/I since before it became major news, and expresses interest in more of the account's research.

Crozier's statement that he had already been covering A/I is consistent with his October 2025 reporting. The significance of this reply is therefore narrower than a source-attribution claim: it documents direct outreach and solicitation of additional research in August 2026.

ASSESSMENT

What changed in the investigation.

JUL 16 · 2025AntifaWatch publicly names A/I as shared infrastructure.Documented by preserved screenshot.
OCT 16 · 2025Crozier publishes his A/I / NoBlogs infrastructure investigation.Documented by the published Daily Caller article.
AUG 30 · 2026Crozier publicly says he contacted AntifaWatch seeking more A/I research.Documented by preserved screenshot.

Supportable conclusion: AntifaWatch was publicly using an A/I-as-shared-infrastructure frame before Crozier's October 2025 report, and Crozier later directly solicited more A/I research from AntifaWatch.

Not proven: that AntifaWatch supplied Crozier's October 2025 reporting; that any particular claim in Crozier's work originated with AntifaWatch; that AntifaWatch, Crozier or another outside actor caused the U.S. designation; or that the public similarities reveal coordination rather than overlapping research interests. Those remain separate questions requiring source or government records.

Records that would move this from chronology to transmission

  • The Aug. 2026 email or DM exchange Crozier publicly said he initiated with AntifaWatch.
  • Earlier communications, if any, between Crozier and AntifaWatch concerning A/I, NoBlogs or Rose City Counter-Info.
  • Government communications or analytical products that cite or circulate AntifaWatch material concerning A/I.
  • Source notes or published attribution showing where the specific shared-infrastructure / foreign-designation framing entered Crozier's 2025 reporting.
