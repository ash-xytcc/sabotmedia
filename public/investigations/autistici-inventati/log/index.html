September 5, 2026

A/I says Banca Etica will terminate its account and remaining donated funds will be unavailable.

Autistici/Inventati published a Sept. 5 press release saying Banca Etica informed the AI ODV association during a Sept. 4 video call that it will unilaterally terminate the banking relationship and that money still in the account, including donations from individuals, organizations and collectives, will no longer be available to the association.

A/I says this prevents it from meeting administrative commitments, paying service providers and providing services, and says the association intends to use available legal remedies to challenge the decision and regain access to donated funds.

The development is stronger than Banca Etica's earlier public position. In its prior statement, the bank said the account had been temporarily suspended, that it was seeking advice and institutional guidance, and that closure was probable but that it was trying to find a way to avoid it. The Sept. 5 A/I release says the bank has now communicated a termination decision.
