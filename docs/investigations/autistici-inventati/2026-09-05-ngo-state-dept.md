# Sept. 5, 2026 — Andy Ngo tags State Department over Sabot Media reporting

Andy Ngo publicly tagged the U.S. State Department in a post accusing Sabot Media of “providing support” to Autistici/Inventati and calling the publication A/I’s “U.S. propaganda wing.” The post quote-posted Kyle Shideler, who described Sabot as a Washington-based anarchist and Antifa collective “leading the media campaign in support of” A/I.
