## What can be established

The post URL is a Hudson Crozier X status from Sept. 1, 2026 and was identified contemporaneously as linking to Sabot Media. That places Crozier publicly directing readers toward Sabot during the same period in which he was reporting on Autistici/Inventati and NoBlogs.

Two days earlier, on Aug. 30, Crozier published a Washington Examiner investigation titled “How a leftist network used by terrorists operated in plain sight for years.” The full article names the NoBlogs site “Sabot Media,” links to an archived copy of Sabot’s Aug. 27 article and quotes Sabot’s discussion of A/I’s infrastructure resilience and the wider effects of U.S. sanctions on banks, hosts, registrars, infrastructure providers and supporters.

## Reporting significance

The significance is the sequence, not the mere fact that a reporter linked a publication. By Sept. 1, Crozier was publicly pointing readers toward Sabot after already naming and quoting Sabot in his Aug. 30 Examiner article.

Crozier is also part of the earlier source lineage documented in this investigation: his Oct. 16, 2025 Daily Caller reporting quoted Kyle Shideler, and Shideler later cited Crozier’s reporting in Senate testimony that named A/I and NoBlogs.

Four days after Crozier’s Sept. 1 post, Shideler publicly described Sabot as leading a media campaign “in support of” A/I, and Andy Ngo quote-posted Shideler while accusing Sabot of “providing support” to A/I and tagging the U.S. State Department.

This record does not treat Crozier’s link as evidence of coordination, endorsement or shared intent. It documents that Sabot had entered the public sourcing and commentary trail around the A/I story before the Sept. 5 allegations directed at Sabot.
